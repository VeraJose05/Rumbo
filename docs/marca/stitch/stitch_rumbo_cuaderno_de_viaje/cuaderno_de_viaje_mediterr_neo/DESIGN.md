## Brand & Style

The design system translates the intimate, tactile feeling of a well-traveled field notebook into a functional digital product. It draws from Mediterranean travel lore—sun-bleached terra cotta, weathered ceramic tiles, olive groves, and pressed botanical keepsakes. It rejects sterile tourist guidebooks in favor of a playful, grounded travel companion: the insider friend who sketches a shortcut on a café napkin.

The core aesthetic bridges tactile editorial journaling with modern utility. Structural surfaces behave like physical paper tiers, train passes, and expedition logs. Interactive focal points use postage silhouettes, perforated ticket edges, stamp impressions, and subtle stationery accents to reward exploration and discovery while maintaining rigorous digital ergonomics.

## Colors

The palette balances warm earth minerals, historic Mediterranean architectural pigments, and artisanal paper tones:

- **Primary (`#C4553A`)**: Sun-baked terracotta. Serves as the primary brand identifier, badge highlights, and thematic anchor.
- **Primary Interactive (`#A8432B`)**: Deepened terracotta specifically reserved for actionable tap targets and primary buttons, guaranteeing sufficient contrast against paper backgrounds while pairing with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Secondary (`#1E4C7A`)**: Andalusian ceramic tile blue (*Azul azulejo*). Used for transit itineraries, coastal chapters, structural accents, and secondary interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#F2B544`)**: Saffron mustard. Applied to achievement stars, gamified passport stamps, progress accents, and contextual notices.
- **Accent Olive (`#4F6B3F`)**: Earthy olive grove green for nature trails, completed routes, and ecological recommendations.
- **Background Paper Canvas (`#FAF7F2`)**: Off-white, sunlit sand tone that prevents eye fatigue and establishes a physical paper presence.
- **Surface Paper Tint (`#FFF6EC`)**: Warm cream paper used for stacked cards, passport entry stubs, and distinct overlay surfaces.
- **Ink / Neutral (`#2B2622`)**: Warm charcoal ink for typography, line rules, and stamp outlines, avoiding clinical digital pitch blacks.

## Typography

The type system balances bookish narrative warmth with clean, legible interface mechanics.

- **Headlines & Narrative (`Literata`)**: Provides an authentic bookmaker feel. The organic serifs and warm contours establish the voice of an explorer’s chronicle, imbuing destination names and quest milestones with cultural substance.
- **Interface & Content (`Inter`)**: Guarantees legibility across dense travel logistics, schedule timelines, search inputs, and micro-labels.
- **Editorial Annotations & Marginalia**: Handcrafted notes, arrows, and margin curiosities use informal handwriting styling to complement Literata and Inter without disrupting interface scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point base spatial grid, tailored to mobile-first hand navigation while accommodating tablet travel scrapbooks and desktop trip planners:

- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Single-column stacks mimic vertical journal pages and travel passports.
- **Tablet (768px–1023px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates two-page open notebook presentations (map viewport on the left, daily log cards on the right).
- **Desktop (≥1024px)**: 12-column layout with a centered maximum container width of `1200px`, preserving the intimate feel of a personal study desk without excessive line lengths.

Rhythm is deliberate: generous negative space around narrative headers allows parchment textures to breathe, while itinerary rows and checklist elements adopt tight, functional `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids clinical digital drop shadows in favor of warm, tactile paper layering:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#FAF7F2` parchment base. Flat, matte, texture-bearing.
- **Level 1 (Card & Paper Sheets)**: `#FFF6EC` surface resting with a soft ambient shadow tinted with warm charcoal and terracotta tones: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(43, 38, 34, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(196, 85, 58, 0.04)`. Outlined with a delicate hairline border: `1px solid rgba(43, 38, 34, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Passes, Modals & Stamped Overlays)**: Distinct elevated elements such as tickets, bottom sheets, and passport stamps: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 38, 34, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(196, 85, 58, 0.06)`.
- **Stamp & Ink Imprints**: Gamified stamps, seals, and transit punches sit pressed into surfaces rather than floating, using slight inner shadows or subtle rotational offsets (`-1deg` to `2deg`) to evoke human hand application.

## Shapes

The foundational corner radius scale conforms to Level 2 (Rounded), conveying organic, handmade paper cards rather than rigid digital blocks:

- **Base components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges)**: `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Containers & Scrapbook Cards (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (16px).
- **Dialogs & Bottom Sheets (`rounded-xl`)**: `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Stamps & Washi Tape Embellishments**: Pill corners (`9999px`) or irregular scalloped cutouts along edges.
- **Perforated Ticket Motifs**: Inward radial corner notches (`mask-image: radial-gradient(...)`) positioned along the divider seams of boarding tickets and train vouchers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Built with `#A8432B` background and `#FFFFFF` bold Inter typography. Roundedness is `0.5rem`. Hover and active states press subtly inward (`transform: translateY(1px)`).
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#FAF7F2` with a `1.5px` border in `#1E4C7A` and text in `#1E4C7A`.
- **Ghost / Journal Link**: Text in `#C4553A` with a handwritten-styled underline, responding with a warm cream background tint on hover.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Shaped with `0.5rem` radii. Unselected chips sit on `#FAF7F2` with subtle `rgba(43, 38, 34, 0.12)` borders and `#2B2622` text.
- Active chips take `#1E4C7A` or `#4F6B3F` with white typography, accompanied by a small ink-dot indicator.

### Cards (The "Scrapbook Folio")
- Formed with `#FFF6EC` backgrounds, `1rem` corner rounding, and Level 1 warm shadow tinting.
- Itinerary cards feature dashed perforated border breaks (`border-top: 1.5px dashed rgba(43, 38, 34, 0.2)`).
- Optional decorative "washi tape" strip centered on top borders using semi-translucent saffron `#F2B544` with angled edges.

### Lists & Itinerary Milestones
- Connected by dotted vertical ink lines (`rgba(43, 38, 34, 0.2)`).
- Route waypoints use circular olive `#4F6B3F` or terracotta `#C4553A` nodes containing numbered steps or station glyphs.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Surface: `#FAF7F2` with a `1px` border of `rgba(43, 38, 34, 0.2)`. Corner radius: `0.5rem`.
- Focus state: Replaces border with a crisp `2px` stroke of `#1E4C7A` and a faint terracotta outer glow. Placeholder text in muted warm charcoal (`rgba(43, 38, 34, 0.5)`).

### Checkboxes & Exploration Toggles
- Custom square markers with `4px` corner radii. Unchecked: `1.5px` stroke in charcoal ink. Checked: Filled with `#4F6B3F` carrying a white checkmark reminiscent of a pen stroke.

### Passport Stamps & Gamification Badges
- Hexagonal, circular, or scalloped borders displaying destination heraldry, regional dates, or route completions. Rendered with slight textural opacity (`0.92`) and micro-rotations (`-2deg` to `+3deg`) to recreate hand-stamped ink impressions.
